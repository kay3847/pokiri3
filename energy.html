Our Sustainable Energy Mix

Committed to net-zero emissions by 2050 through responsible energy generation

Powering Pokiri Responsibly

Our diverse energy portfolio balances reliability, affordability, and environmental responsibility. We're continuously increasing our renewable energy percentage while maintaining grid stability.

Natural Gas

60% of our energy mix

Our natural gas plants provide reliable base load power with lower emissions than traditional coal plants.

Solar

25% of our energy mix

Our solar farms harness Pokiri's abundant sunshine, with battery storage to extend availability.

Wind

15% of our energy mix

Wind turbines in our northern region complement solar generation during evening hours.

Our Path to Net Zero by 2050

Strategic Renewable Expansion

We're investing $50 million annually to increase our renewable capacity, targeting:

  • 40% renewable mix by 2025
  • 70% renewable mix by 2035
  • 100% carbon-neutral by 2050

Grid Modernization

Our smart grid initiatives will enable:

  • Advanced demand response programs
  • Distributed energy resource integration
  • Real-time energy monitoring for members
